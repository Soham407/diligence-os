Problem statement

Financial intelligence in India is fragmented across too many surfaces.

Users are not missing information. They are missing a system that can resolve the entity, fetch the right local sources, and turn them into a report they can trust without doing the assembly by hand.

Retail investor Find the gist fast.

A 200-page annual report or a dense earnings transcript should not require a half hour of page-jumping before the user understands what changed.

Wants the summary, the shift in management tone, and the red flags in under two minutes.
Consultant / wealth manager Assemble a client-ready dossier.

The pain is not analysis alone. It is the manual stitching together of filings, screenshots, transcripts, and comparison notes into something presentable.

Wants a branded, auditable report that can be used in a client call the same day.
Agency operator Protect exclusivity by design.

Client research must stay isolated. Shared caches are useful, but not when they compromise the promise of white-label delivery.

Wants structural separation, not just a policy promise.
Product

Three tiers, one architecture.

The system supports retail, B2B SaaS, and agency workflows in parallel. The frontend changes tone by tier, but the upstream model stays the same: resolve, ingest, reason, verify, export.

Retail tier

A freemium summary flow for investors. Paste a ticker or company name, then get a concise readout with sentiment shift, red flags, and citations.

B2B SaaS tier

A desktop dashboard for consultants, advisors, and wealth managers. It runs a deeper dossier, tracks quotas, and keeps exports in one branded place.

Agency tier

A project-scoped workbench for client research. White-label branding and cache bypass are structural, so exclusivity is enforced by the system itself.

Trust layer

Every claim should point back to a source document.

The UI should not hide provenance. It should surface citations, source hashes, and verification state so the output can be defended in a client call or audit.
